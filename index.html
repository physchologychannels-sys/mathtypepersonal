How to type (cheat sheet)

Fraction: 8/9 · with brackets (a+b)/(c-d) · stacked fractions (8/9)/(1/2)
Power: x^2, x^(n+1) · Subscript: x_1 · Root: sqrt(x), sqrt[3](x)
Sum: sum_(i=1)^n i · Integral: int_0^1 x^2 dx · Limit: lim_(x->0) sin(x)/x
Matrix: mat(1,2;3,4) · Absolute value: abs(x) · Vector/bar/hat: vec(v) bar(x) hat(x)
Symbols: -> → · <= ≤ · >= ≥ · != ≠ · +- ± · * × · pi π · alpha α · inf ∞ · deg °
Plain words inside math: put them in quotes, like "area" = pi r^2
Piecewise: cases(x, x>=0; -x, x<0) · Binomial: binom(n,k) · Matrices: pmat(…) round, bmat square, vmat determinant bars, Bmat curly
Double/triple integral: iint_(D) f dA, iiint · Big union/intersection: bigcup_(i=1)^n, bigcap · Floor/ceiling/norm: floor(x) ceil(x) norm(v)
Accents: overline(AB) underline(x) tilde(x) dot(x) ddot(x) · Over/under: over(x,y) under(x,y) · Labelled arrow: arrow(top,bottom)

Best for Word: click “Open in Word (.docx)”. The equations in that file are real Word equations you can edit. “Copy as picture” pastes anywhere, but it is an image.
